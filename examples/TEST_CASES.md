# Test cases

1. **Simple round trip**
   "Rome to New York, 12–19 November, 1 person, cabin bag only. Find the cheapest practical option."

2. **Flexible date search**
   "Milan to Los Angeles for 10–14 days in February. I can leave any day in the first two weeks. Checked bag required."

3. **Nearby-airport hack**
   "I live near Pescara and need to reach London in October. Check Pescara, Rome, Bologna and any sensible train-to-airport positioning options."

4. **Booking timing**
   "I want to fly Rome–Tokyo in late March. Is it better to book now or wait? Show the evidence/assumptions."

5. **Hidden-city explanation**
   "Explain whether a hidden-city itinerary could reduce the cost from Madrid to New York and what the risks are."

6. **Self-transfer**
   "Compare direct flights with a separate-ticket connection via London. I care about total cost but do not want a high missed-connection risk."

7. **Bad-data resistance**
   Verify that the assistant does not invent a fare when live web results are unavailable.
